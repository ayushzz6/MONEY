      How it ranks: strength = the move since yesterday's close in ATRs × how straight today's path was × where the price sits in the day's range; choppy stocks (straightness under 25%) score zero. An order block is the last opposite candle before a 5-minute close through the last swing high or low. Picks: the top 3 on each side, at most 2 from one sector, never a stock that moved over 3 ATR or 8% (event risk). Backtested on the last 2 years: buying or selling the first retest of these zones lost about ₹14,000 a month on ₹1 lakh after costs (report), so use the board as a screener, not as trade signals. Heads-up (a break forming, a zone just given, the price near Z) and the first-stage entry are not backtested yet. Analytics only, not investment advice.

            Bulk and block deals

            Who bought or sold big. A bulk deal is one client trading over 0.5% of a company's shares in a day; a block deal is a single trade of ₹10 crore or more. NSE posts them after each session. A buyer and seller with the same name on the same day is usually an intraday trading firm, not an investor. Past sessions show at most 70 bulk deals each (NSE's limit); the latest session is complete.

            Your risk rules

              To change a rule, edit configs/trading.json and restart the bot. They are not editable here, so a stray click cannot loosen a limit.

              How the bot works, in one minute

              1. Before the open, it scans the NIFTY 500 and picks the ~50 stocks worth watching. It also re-tests every strategy and reads the news, results calendar and big deals.
              2. From 09:15 it reads a new 5-minute candle for every watchlist stock. When a strategy's rule matches, that is a signal.
              3. Before acting, it sets the stop-loss and target, sizes the trade so a stop-out costs at most your limit, checks every risk rule, and replays the setup in the Time Machine.
              4. In Paper mode it trades with practice money by itself. In LIVE mode every entry waits for you to press Approve; exits happen automatically.
              5. It closes everything by the flat-by time (15:10) and writes the day into the journal.

              Your daily routine

              1. Start the bot: ./run.sh in the MONEYV2 folder, then open this page.
              2. Glance at Home: mode, market status, and anything under "Needs your attention".
              3. During the day, approve or reject trades (LIVE only) from the yellow bar at the top.
              4. If anything looks wrong, press Pause new trades. Exit all positions closes everything at market.
              5. After the close, review Trades → History.

              Safety built in

              • Paper by defaultNo real order is ever sent unless TRADING_MODE=LIVE is set in .env.
              • Your yes for every entryIn LIVE mode each new trade waits for Approve and expires if you do not answer.
              • Daily loss limitAfter the day's loss limit, no new trades until tomorrow.
              • Local onlyThis page only works on this computer (127.0.0.1), and other websites cannot press its buttons.

              Words you will see

              Signals are rule matches, not predictions. Backtests describe the past, not the future, and are not investment advice. A stop-loss is an order, not a guarantee: a gap can fill worse. Most intraday traders lose money.
